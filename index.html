How to Use this Site

  • Favourite Stations: Tap the heart icon in the search dropdown to favourite a station. On touchscreens, simply long-press any station to pin/unpin it. Favourites appear right at the top for quick access.
  • Instant Search: Type any station name to filter results. Aliases (such as Majestic, KBS, or SBC) work seamlessly behind the scenes.
  • Live Fare Highlight: A green dot automatically highlights whichever Smart Card rate (Peak 5% off or Non-Peak 10% off) is active right now.
  • Corridor Selection: Use Via Station to force journeys through specific transfer hubs like Majestic or RV Road.
  • Timetable Hub: Open the menu to check first and terminal-specific last train departure timings across all metro corridors.
Namma Metro Network: Purple Line, Green Line, Yellow Line, and Phase 2 Pink Line (Coming Soon). Click Network Map on the header for the complete network diagram.
